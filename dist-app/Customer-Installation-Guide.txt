Welcome to Crazy Crunch! You can install our application directly onto your
Android phone, iPhone, or Computer in under 30 seconds — 100% Free, with
NO Google Play Store login or download wait times.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📱 1. HOW TO INSTALL ON ANDROID PHONES (CHROME / BRAVE / SAMSUNG INTERNET)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Open Google Chrome on your Android smartphone.
Step 2: Navigate to our official Crazy Crunch link:
        http://<YOUR_SERVER_IP>:3001   (or your live HTTPS web URL)

Step 3: Look for the banner at the bottom or top that says:
        "Install Crazy Crunch" or "Add to Home screen".
        Tap the "Install" or "Install App" button.

Step 4: If you do not see the banner automatically:
        a) Tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Google Chrome.
        b) Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
        c) Tap "Install" to confirm.

Step 5: The "Crazy Crunch" app icon will now appear on your phone's home screen
        and app drawer, exactly like any regular app! You can launch it in
        full-screen mode anytime with one tap.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🍏 2. HOW TO INSTALL ON APPLE iPHONE (SAFARI BROWSER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Open Apple Safari on your iPhone.
Step 2: Go to the Crazy Crunch link.
Step 3: Tap the "Share" button at the bottom of the screen (the square icon
        with an arrow pointing up ⎋).
Step 4: Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (➕).
Step 5: Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.
Step 6: The Crazy Crunch app icon is now saved on your iPhone Home Screen!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
💻 3. HOW TO INSTALL ON LAPTOP / DESKTOP (WINDOWS & MAC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Open Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
Step 2: Go to the Crazy Crunch link.
Step 3: Click the small "Install" icon (🖥️ or ⊕) in the browser address bar.
Step 4: Click "Install" to create a desktop app window.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🛒 HOW TO PLACE AN ORDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Browse freshly prepared homemade snacks (Chakli, Sev, Khari, Laddus, etc.).
2. Select your quantity and click "Add to Cart" or "Buy Now".
3. Tap the Cart icon and click "Proceed to Checkout".
4. Enter your Name, Phone Number, Delivery Address, Area, City, and PIN code.
5. Choose Cash on Delivery (COD) or instant Scan & Pay via UPI.
6. Click "Confirm Order". You will receive an instant order confirmation
   with your unique Order Reference ID (e.g., CC-2026-XXXX).
